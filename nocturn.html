Our Story

Coffee as it should be.

We started Nocturn out of a garage in Southeast Portland because the specialty scene felt gatekept. Nine years in, we're still obsessed with the same thing: pulling the best flavor out of every bean we touch — and paying the farmers who grew them a wage that reflects that.

  • Direct trade only. We know every farm by name.
  • Roasted Tuesday. Shipped Wednesday. On your table by Friday.
  • Compostable bags. Even the valve.
